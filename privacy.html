Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy covers the WENIK website, web app and mobile app (Android package: com.wenik.app), operated by WENIK. Contact WENIK at wenik.co@gmail.com about personal data or privacy requests.

Information we process

When you register, we process your name, mobile number, account identifier, authentication information and acceptance of terms, privacy and optional marketing preferences. Your account also contains points, purchases and partner transactions, reward requests or reservations, and messages. We process promotional impression and click events and technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. Information you send to support is used to respond to you.

Location and device permissions

You can search and choose an area manually. In the current mobile app, “Use my location” requests foreground location permission and uses your coordinates on your device to suggest a nearby area. This feature does not send your precise coordinates to the WENIK database or track your location in the background. Opening “View map” sends the selected coordinates to Google Maps. Camera access, where requested for QR scanning, is used for that feature. You can refuse or revoke permissions in your device settings.

How information is used and shared

We use information to authenticate accounts, show partners and offers, maintain points and purchase records, fulfil reward requests, provide customer support and prevent misuse. Relevant account and transaction information is available to authorised WENIK staff and the partner involved in a purchase or reward so they can provide the service. Hosting, database, authentication and SMS providers process information needed to deliver these services; WENIK uses Supabase and Vercel infrastructure. These services may process data outside your country. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect accounts and the service.

Promotions and external links

WENIK displays partner promotions. Optional marketing preferences are recorded when supplied during registration; contact us to change them. External map, social media and messaging links open third-party services governed by their own privacy policies.

Storage, security and retention

The app stores session information locally to keep you signed in. The website uses browser storage for account sessions and service preferences. Access controls and encrypted network connections help protect information. We retain account information while needed to provide the service. After an approved deletion request, personal account data is deleted or anonymised, except records required for accounting, legal obligations, dispute resolution or fraud prevention. Retained records are restricted to those purposes; retention depends on the applicable requirement.

Your choices and account deletion

You can request access, correction or deletion of your information by contacting us. For account deletion, follow the account deletion instructions. Requests are handled manually and require verification of account ownership. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Children and updates

If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us so we can investigate and address it. We will update this page when our data practices change.
