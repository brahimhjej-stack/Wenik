Delete your WENIK account

You can request deletion of your WENIK account and associated personal data for the website and mobile app (com.wenik.app).

Request deletion

  1. Email wenik.co@gmail.com with the subject “WENIK account deletion request”.
  2. Include your registered mobile number or WENIK member ID, and state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. WENIK support will verify account ownership and respond about processing the request. Do not send passwords, SMS codes or identity documents in your initial email.

What happens to your data

After verification and approval, your account and associated personal profile data are deleted or anonymised. Transaction and redemption records may be retained where required for accounting, legal obligations, disputes or fraud prevention, with access restricted to those purposes. The retention period depends on the applicable requirement. Support will explain any records that must be retained when processing your request.

This is a manual support process. Sending an email does not instantly delete the account. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account.
